# Experiment Design

## Hypothesis

- At least 1 of 3 contacted inn owners will find a guest communication audit valuable enough to pay for or seriously pilot.

## Method

- Contact 10 small inns.
- Offer a short call and sample audit.
- Deliver one revised arrival message and one local recommendation card.

## Participants Or Audience

- Independent inns or B&Bs with 3-20 rooms.

## Success Criteria

- 3 calls booked.
- 1 paid or high-commitment pilot.
- Clear before/after feedback from the owner.

## Timebox

- 2 weeks.

## Risks

- Direct outreach may be ignored.
- Owners may be too busy for calls.
