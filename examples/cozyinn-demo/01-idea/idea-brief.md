# Idea Brief

## One-Sentence Idea

- CozyInn helps small independent inns improve guest arrival instructions and local recommendations without hiring a marketer.

## Who It Might Help

- Independent inn and B&B owners with limited staff.

## Why Now

- Guess: Guests increasingly expect clear digital instructions and curated local tips.

## Desired Outcome

- Learn whether owners value a simple service that improves the guest pre-arrival experience.

## Evidence Labels

- `Fact`: This is a fictional demo.
- `Assumption`: Small inns have inconsistent guest communication.
- `Guess`: Owners may prefer a done-for-you service over another SaaS dashboard.
- `Opinion`: The idea is more credible as a concierge service first.
- `Preference`: Keep the first experiment no-code.
