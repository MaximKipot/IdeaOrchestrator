# Anti-Vision

## This Should Not Become

- A full property management system.
- A generic AI chatbot without hospitality quality control.
- A marketplace for local activities.

## Rejected Directions

| Direction | Why Rejected | Revisit Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking engine | Too large and crowded for first experiment | Only if owners ask repeatedly |
| Guest chatbot | Support and brand risk too high | After manual content proves useful |
