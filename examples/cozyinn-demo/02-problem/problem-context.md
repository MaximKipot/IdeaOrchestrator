# Problem Context

## Problem Statement

- Assumption: Small inn owners want guests to arrive prepared and feel cared for, but guest communication is often assembled from old emails, PDFs, and ad hoc messages.

## Current Alternatives

| Alternative | Who Uses It | What Works | What Fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manual emails | Inn owners | Flexible and free | Inconsistent and time-consuming |
| Property management tools | Larger operators | Integrated workflows | May be too heavy for tiny inns |
| Printed binders | On-site guests | Simple | Not useful before arrival |

## Pain Or Cost

- Guess: Repeated guest questions create owner workload.
- Guess: Confusing arrival instructions can hurt reviews.

## Evidence

| Claim | Classification | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing hospitality messaging tools exist. | Fact | Demo desk research |
| Tiny inns may avoid complex software. | Assumption | Demo reasoning |
