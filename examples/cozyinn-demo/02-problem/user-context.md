# User Context

## Potential Users

| User Segment | Situation | Need | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small independent inn owner | Handles guest communication personally | Better messages with low setup effort | Medium |
| Part-time B&B host | Limited time and inconsistent process | Simple templates and guidance | Low |

## Jobs To Be Done

- When: a guest books a stay
- I want: clear arrival and local information sent automatically or easily
- So I can: reduce questions and improve the stay

## Open User Questions

- What do owners already send before arrival?
- Do they see guest messaging as painful enough to pay for?
