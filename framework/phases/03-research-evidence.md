# Phase 03: Research Evidence

## Purpose

Ground the idea in evidence before strategy and MVP choices.

## Research Mode

Pick one mode before detailed research. The user may override the suggestion.

| Mode | Best For | Required Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Founder Assumption Audit` | Early ideas based mostly on founder belief. | Founder assumptions, validation/falsification signals, and smallest evidence steps. |
| `End-User Questionnaire` | Ideas where target users or buyers can be reached. | Interview or survey questions mapped to assumptions. |
| `Mixed` | Most business, product, service, and market-facing ideas. | Both, kept concise. |
| `Assumption-led` | Momentum matters and the riskiest assumptions are clearer than the full research agenda. | High-risk assumptions, decision-relevant research, deferred research risk, and smallest validation step. |

Every mode must include a market scan for present solutions, substitutes, current workarounds, and comparable alternatives. `Assumption-led` narrows the research scope; it does not remove the market scan, evidence labels, or deferred-risk logging.

## Research Decision Packets

When research exists to answer a specific choice, create `03-research/decision-packet.md` or a named packet and follow `framework/rules/research-decision-packets.md`. Decision packets should include direct evidence, analogous evidence, counter-evidence, confidence, recommendation, and what would change the decision.

## Questions

- What must be true for this idea to work?
- Which research mode fits best: Founder Assumption Audit, End-User Questionnaire, Mixed, or Assumption-led?
- What decision question should this research packet answer?
- What comparable products, services, behaviors, or precedents exist?
- What evidence supports or weakens the idea?
- What contrary evidence or present solution challenges the idea?
- What research depth is appropriate?

## Blocking Conditions

- No research has been done.
- Present solutions or substitutes have not been checked.
- The idea touches regulated, safety-sensitive, legal, financial, or medical areas and uncertainty is not explicit.

## Done When

- Sources and evidence are logged.
- A research mode is recorded.
- Market scan findings are logged.
- Any decision-relevant research packet is linked to the decision log or open question it supports.
- Challenge findings are logged.
- Facts are separated from assumptions and guesses.
- The next recommended skill is `idea-o-04-risks`.
