# Phase 02: Problem Context

## Purpose

Clarify the problem, user context, current alternatives, and why the idea may matter.

- `00-control/current-state.md`

## Questions

- Who experiences the problem?
- What do they do today?
- What makes the problem painful, costly, slow, risky, or emotionally important?
- What would happen if nothing changed?

## Blocking Conditions

- No plausible user or beneficiary is identified.
- The problem is only a solution description.

## Done When

- The problem is described from the user's perspective.
- At least one current alternative is recorded.
- The next recommended skill is `idea-o-03-research`.
