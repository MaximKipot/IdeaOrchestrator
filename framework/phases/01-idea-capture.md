# Phase 01: Idea Capture

## Purpose

Capture the raw idea without prematurely judging it.

## Read

- `00-control/current-state.md`
- `01-idea/raw-notes.md`

## Write

- `01-idea/idea-brief.md`
- `01-idea/raw-notes.md`
- `00-control/current-state.md`

## Questions

- What is the idea?
- Who might it help?
- What triggered the idea?
- What would make it worth exploring?

## Blocking Conditions

- The idea has no clear action, audience, or context.

## Skip Behavior

Skipping detailed capture is allowed only if a short idea brief exists. Log the risk that later phases may optimize around the wrong idea.

## Done When

- A concise idea brief exists.
- Raw notes are preserved.
- The next recommended phase is `problem-definition`.
