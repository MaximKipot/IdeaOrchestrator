# Challenge Pass

Use this as the framework's devil's advocate check. The goal is to improve the idea, not to argue for its own sake.

## When To Run

Run a challenge pass in these phases:

- `03 Research Evidence`: look for contrary evidence, substitutes, and present solutions.
- `04 Assumptions Risks`: identify failure modes and fragile assumptions.
- `08 MVP Experiments`: test whether each experiment could mislead.
- `09 Decisions Roadmap`: challenge the selected path before committing.
- `11 Review Pivot`: challenge continuation after new evidence arrives.

## Questions

- What would make this idea fail?
- What evidence weakens the current direction?
- What present solution, workaround, or substitute could make this unnecessary?
- Which assumption is most fragile?
- What would make us stop, pause, or pivot?
- Could the next experiment produce misleading positive evidence?

## Required Output

Record the result in the current phase file:

| Challenge | Evidence Or Reason | Severity | Response | Stop / Pivot Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Rules

- Label claims as Fact, Assumption, Guess, Opinion, Preference, or Decision.
- Do not delete the challenged idea or rejected alternative.
- If the challenge is accepted, update risks, assumptions, rejected options, or decisions.
- If the challenge is dismissed, record why.
