# Research Decision Packets

Use a research decision packet when evidence is being gathered to answer a specific decision question.

## Format

Create `03-research/decision-packet.md` or a named packet such as `03-research/decision-packet-<topic>.md` with:

- Decision question.
- Decision owner and date needed.
- Direct evidence.
- Analogous evidence.
- Counter-evidence.
- Confidence: Low | Medium | High.
- Recommendation.
- What would change the decision.
- Decision-log update needed.

## Rules

- Label each claim as `Fact`, `Assumption`, `Guess`, `Opinion`, `Preference`, or `Decision`.
- Include direct evidence when available; use analogous evidence only when direct evidence is unavailable or incomplete.
- Include counter-evidence even when the recommendation is strong.
- If the recommendation becomes an accepted decision, update `00-control/decision-log.md` and link back to the packet.
- If evidence is insufficient, log the missing evidence in `00-control/open-questions.md` or the research gap section.
